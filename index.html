Features

Everything you need for a clean investigation

Built into one overlay so you can stop guessing and start identifying.

👁️

Ghost ESP

Draws a live outline and distance marker on the ghost through walls, so you always know where it is roaming and when it starts a hunt.

💡

Fullbright

Lifts the entire map out of the dark with a single toggle — no flashlight battery drain, no flickering, no missed evidence in the basement.

📊

Sanity & Evidence Readout

A compact HUD shows your exact sanity percentage and every piece of evidence collected so far, updating in real time as the round progresses.

🧠

Ghost-Type Guesser

Cross-references your collected evidence and observed behavior against all ghost types and suggests the most likely candidate with a confidence score.

🎯

Teleport-to-Ghost Keybinds

Bind a key to jump straight to the ghost's current position — perfect for fast evidence checks or a quick exit when the hunt starts.
